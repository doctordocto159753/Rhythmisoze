# Realistic instrument listening test

The sample engine is technically verifiable; preference is not. This protocol
is the release gate for US-INST-006 and prevents an implementation detail from
being reported as a subjective success.

## Test material

Use one fixed 12–20 second hummed melody with short attacks, held notes, a small
range leap and both soft/loud phrases. Keep the transcription, retouch amount,
tempo, volume and reverb identical. Render A/B pairs for each of the six sample
instruments: recorded engine versus its procedural fallback. Randomise which
version is A.

## Participants

Minimum five listeners, including at least two people who regularly play or
record an acoustic instrument. Headphones or desktop speakers; no phone speaker
scores count in this desktop-first phase.

## Current result

Not yet scored by human listeners. Automated and browser validation must not be
reported as passing this subjective gate. The in-product preview provides the
final A/B surface for the owner/listeners.
