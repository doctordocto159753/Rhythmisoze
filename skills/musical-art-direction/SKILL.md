# Skill — Musical Art Direction

## Use when
Designing or revising Rhythmisoze's visual identity, page composition, visual language, creative direction or any user-facing screen.

## Goal
Produce a coherent musical instrument, not a generic product UI decorated with music imagery.

## Required process
1. Restate the musical/user state being designed.
2. Identify the dominant sensory relationship: pulse, pitch, energy, transformation, material or instrument character.
3. Decide global composition before local components.
4. Produce at least two materially different compositions before converging on one.
5. Check Persian and English layouts before finalizing.
6. Check the screen in still form and during state transition.
7. Audit the whole screen after every local change.

## Quality bar
- One clear focal hierarchy.
- Spacing and scale feel intentional at page and component levels.
- Visual motifs recur with variation rather than copy-paste.
- Music behavior influences time, space or material without turning into literal notation everywhere.
- Visual identity survives without 3D.
- 3D, if present, deepens the same thesis.

## Avoid
- card grids by default;
- decorative waveforms;
- generic gradients;
- arbitrary rounded rectangles;
- neon music visualizer clichés;
- “premium SaaS” luxury spacing divorced from musical use;
- isolated component beautification that weakens the whole.

## Deliverable checklist
- visual thesis;
- composition diagram;
- typography/material rules;
- key states;
- motion note;
- RTL/LTR check;
- whole-screen audit.
